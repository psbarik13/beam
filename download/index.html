Transfer tools

Two ways to move files between a computer and a phone.

Beam

Sends a file through a stream of QR codes, screen to camera. No network at all, so it works where Wi-Fi sharing is blocked. Open it on both devices.

Open Beam Download to run locally

On a phone, use this page's https link so the camera works. On a computer, the download includes start-beam, which opens Beam with camera access.

Courier

Shares a folder on your PC or Mac with your phone over Wi-Fi: upload, download, make folders, and stream videos. Scan a QR code to connect.

Download for Windows and Mac

Runs on your computer with Node.js. Unzip, then double-click start.bat (Windows) or start.command (Mac).
